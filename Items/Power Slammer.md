# Crafting

The Power Hammer is crafted using a [[Battery Pack]], [[Hammer]], and a [[Broken Lever]].
# Usage
You can use it to smash thin or weak walls down to reveal secrets in addition to breaking small [[Trunks|trunks]]. These are all the locations we know where you may use the slammer:
* [[Secret Garden]]: Got us an extra third wheel to spin the wind direction pointer thing—allowing us to get access to both sides of the antechamber.
* [[Mining Tunnel]]: Allows permanent access to the Sanctum and reservoir through the basement.
