# Classroom Notes
In the grade 8 [[Classroom]] there's two pictures for affixes and vocabulary.

![[Erajan Affix Pairings.png]]

| Prefix | Meaning           | Suffix | Meaning                    |
| ------ | ----------------- | ------ | -------------------------- |
| AJ     | First             | JA     | Last                       |
| MA     | Small             | AM     | Large                      |
| OV     | Towards           | VO     | Away                       |
| NEV    | New, the start of | VEN    | Old, the end of            |
| EL     | I, me, oneself*   | LE     | A lack of onself*          |
| UL     | You               | LU     | A lack of you, without you |
\* When EL/LE are paired with a verb, "I will" or "I won't" are acceptable definitions. Similar to UL/LU.

![[Erajan Vocabulary.png|431]]

Early Erajan did not use any verbs, so today the rare useage of verbs are comprised almost entirely of loan words and are mainly used to describe physical actions.
