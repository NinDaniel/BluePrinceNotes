## Orrsphere - 3rd Era Map
![[Grade5OrrSphere.png|412]]

Between Corarica and Verra is *The Great Tress*. Eastward is *ajsei*. Southward is *seija*.
### The North Circle
Nothing new?s
### The South Key
There's an island 8 at the bottom of the Seija that connects all continents. It does not appear on the map itself but does in the south key. It connects [[Corarica]], [[Verra]], [[Nuance]], and [[Eraja]].
### Spiral of Stars
The planets are joined together in a spiral going from the outermost planets and spiralling in towards the sun. The order is:
1) Fennmora
2) Veia
3) Dauja
4) Mora
5) Mamora
### The Sacred Circle
8 shapes surround a 9th. Starting at 12o'clock, their order is:
1 → 7 → 4 → 6 → 2 → 8 → 3 → 5

Hovering over the 8th shape is a circle with the words *TOR* and the number 46. The top left quarter has 90 degrees written. The top right quarter has 58 then four 8 degree lines.

## Ettsphere - 3rd Era Map
![[Grade5EttSphere.png|412]]

In the Orinda gulf is *ejsei*. The bottom island of [[Nuance]] is called *Ferther Nuance*. Northward is *innsei*. Eastward is *orrsei*. At the bottom, two moons are shown. The waning crescent moon is titled *Isseclipse*, the waxing crescent moon is titled *Inneclipse*.
## The Seeker Signs
The nations of [[Arch Aries]], [[Orinda Aries]], [[Eraja]], [[Nuance]], [[Verra]], and [[Mora Jai]] are connected to the central [[Corarica]] piece. Each one is connected to its neighbour in the list.
### The Outer Circle
This names the planets. From closest to the sun to furthest they are:
* Mamora — Small 
* Mora — Earth
* Dauja — Ringed like Saturn
* Veia — Large
* Fennmora — Small again
### The Devoted Circle
Circling Mora (or earth), there is a large moon called The Devoted Moon, and a lesser moon orbiting it called The Rogue Moon. The Devoted Moon's orbit around Mora has text reading "devoted orbit — 24 hours".
### The Three of Tides
This depicts the *Three Isles of Orrsei*.
