# Gameplay

The Utility Closet houses a fuse box that alters the behaviour of several rooms and mechanisms of the manor. Namely, it can control:
* The power to [[security doors]]
* The power to the [[Gymnasium]]
* The power to the [[Dark Room]]
* The power to the [[Garage]]
* The power to the [[Cabinet]]
	* This one is broken / missing a lever for some reason
In addition, the Utility Closet also controls the "V.A.C." system, a secret mechanism to unlocking the [[Gemstone Cavern]].
# Secrets

> [!QUOTE] Danger Note found on fuse box
> Toggling of breaker switches may result in unexpected loss of floorplan functionality. Please call D.C. Power if you require assistance.

## Fuse Box Puzzle

In the [[Mail Room]], you can find a note that demonstrates how to operate the VAC switches.

In the [[Office]], you can use a [[terminal]] to access the email system of the estate and read a couple of emails between Gabe, a DCPOWER inc. employee, and [[Herbert S. Sinclair]]. These emails instruct us on how to proceed with the puzzle.
