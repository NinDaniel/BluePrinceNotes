# Gameplay

Security allows you to change the amount of [[security doors|key card doors]] you're expecting to see in the latter ranks. The three settings are:

1) Low — Expecting to see maybe 1 or 2 doors
2) Medium — Expecting to see maybe 3 or 4 doors
3) High — Expecting to see maybe 5 or 6 doors

Importantly, you can also change the operational status of our doors in the case of a power outage. One which we can induce through the panel in the [[Utility Closet]]. If the doors lose power, they can either:

1) Remain closed
2) Forced open

By default, the terminal is set for medium and remain closed. Depending on whether you have the [[Key Card]] [[Items|item]] or access to the [[Utility Closet]], you'll want to change the settings appropriately:

|          | No Item    | Key Card | Utility Closet |
| -------- | ---------- | -------- | -------------- |
| Settings | Low/Either | High/On  | High/Off       |
