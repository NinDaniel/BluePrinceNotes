# Gameplay

Give the statue money and get a blessing 😀 The duration of the blessing depends on how much money has been donated. Below are the different blessings and their effects.

| Name                         | Details                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Blessing of the Dancer       | While drafting, you may pay [[Gems]] to spin floorplans.                                                                                               |
| Blessing of the High Roller  | Each time you draft a [[Yellow Rooms\|Shop]], gain 1 [[Ivory Dice]].<br>Gain 5 extra [[Gold Coins]] each time you roll an [[Ivory Dice]].              |
| Blessing of the Gardener     | Add 8 [[Courtyard\|Courtyards]] to your draft pool.                                                                                                    |
| Blessing of the Tinkerer     | Whenever you draft a Mechanical Room, trigger your current [[Laboratory#^a0f762\|Experiment]].                                                         |
| Blessing of the General      | After you draft a [[Red Rooms\|Red room]], if you have at least 5 different ranks containing [[Red Rooms]], gain 5 [[Gems]].                           |
| Blessing of the Chef         | For each different type of [Spread](obsidian://search?query=["type":Spread]) that you do each day, add an extra dish to [[Dining Room]].               |
| Blessing of the Monk         | If you call it a day in a room in your house, you will have the opportunity to draft that room from the grounds the following day                      |
| Blessing of the Berry Picker | While drafting, instead of picking a floorplan, you can pick a berry. If you do, draft a floorplan at random from your draft pool disregarding rarity. |
