# Gameplay

Give the statue money and get a blessing 😀 The duration of the blessing depends on how much money has been donated. Below are the different blessings and their effects.

You can find the death statue thing here if you buy it from the [[Mount Holly Gift Shop]] and break it open with a [[Hammer]].

You can force which blessing you get by paying its appropriate cost values.

| Name                         | Details                                                                                                                                                | Cost Values (Starts at 3 Days) |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Blessing of the Dancer       | While drafting, you may pay [[Gems]] to spin floorplans.                                                                                               | 1, 17                          |
| Blessing of the High Roller  | Each time you draft a [[Yellow Rooms\|Shop]], gain 1 [[Ivory Dice]].<br>Gain 5 extra [[Gold Coins]] each time you roll an [[Ivory Dice]].              | 3, 19                          |
| Blessing of the Gardener     | Add 8 [[Courtyard\|Courtyards]] to your draft pool.                                                                                                    | 5, 21                          |
| Blessing of the Tinkerer     | Whenever you draft a Mechanical Room, trigger your current [[Laboratory#^a0f762\|Experiment]].                                                         | 7, 23                          |
| Blessing of the General      | After you draft a [[Red Rooms\|Red room]], if you have at least 5 different ranks containing [[Red Rooms]], gain 5 [[Gems]].                           | 9, 25                          |
| Blessing of the Chef         | For each different type of [Spread](obsidian://search?query=["type":Spread]) that you do each day, add an extra dish to [[Dining Room]].               | 11, 27                         |
| Blessing of the Monk         | If you call it a day in a room in your house, you will have the opportunity to draft that room from the grounds the following day                      | 13, 29                         |
| Blessing of the Berry Picker | While drafting, instead of picking a floorplan, you can pick a berry. If you do, draft a floorplan at random from your draft pool disregarding rarity. | 15, 31                         |
